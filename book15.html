Language is the carrier of culture and an essential medium for humans to perceive the world. Context is to expression what water is to fish. A wanderer removed from their specific context is like a fish out of water, unable to breathe freely and fully. Design, as a unique form of visual language, is inherently intertwined with its specific context.

Mandarin is the most widely spoken language in the world. Approximately one in every six people on Earth speaks Mandarin. Designers working within the Mandarin-speaking context operate within an ancient and rich cultural backdrop, using the distinct language of poster design to document and analyze the social and cultural evolution of Mandarin-speaking societies.

On the occasion of the World Design Cities Conference opening, the Mandarin Top 100 Poster Exhibition, as one of the exhibition units, captures subtle shifts and trends in the field of visual communication through poster design. It explores various possibilities within Mandarin visual culture, offering fresh perspectives for design thinking in the industry and inspiring innovative creative forces to contribute to social development.

语言是文化的载体，是人类感知世界的重要媒介。语境之于表达，如同水之于鱼。离开了特定语境的游子，好似鱼儿离了水，无法极致地自由呼吸。设计作为一种特殊的视觉语言，与特定的语境注定有着密不可分的关系。华语是世界上使用者最多的语言。地球上约每六个人中，就有一个使用华语。华语语境中的设计师，在这个古老又丰富的语境下，以海报的特殊设计语言来记录、梳理华语社会与文化的变迁。
