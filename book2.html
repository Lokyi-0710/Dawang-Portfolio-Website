D&I GRAD SHOW 2024

CLIENT: Tongji University

The 2024 graduation exhibition of the College of Design and Innovation at Tongji University was held at the Minghua Sugar Warehouse. The inspiration for the exhibition's main visual, presented in the form of sugar painting, came from the exhibition venue—the sugar warehouse. The team used a robotic arm to print the exhibition's theme, "D&I GRAD SHOW 2024," in syrup, which was then photographed to create the sugar painting poster. Additionally, sugar elements were used to symbolize the students' four years of hard work, culminating in a sweet graduation. Seed paper was used to make name badges, reflecting the dean's message in the exhibition's opening speech: "Plant a seed of design, and it will bloom into a unique and flourishing life!"

2024同济大学设计创意学院的毕业展在明华糖仓举行。以糖画的形式来呈现展览主视觉的灵感来自于展览地点——糖仓。团队借助机械臂使用糖浆打印出展览主题信息“D&I GRAD SHOW 2024”，然后拍摄，进而完成这张糖画海报。另外用糖元素来体现学生们经过四年辛苦学习，最终甜蜜毕业！用种子纸来制作胸卡，体现院长在展览寄语中提到的：“播一粒设计种子，开别样人生繁花！”
